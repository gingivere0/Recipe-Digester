# Lazy Girl High Protein Marry Me Chicken Biscuit Bake
Serves 6. Creamy sauce, cheese, and biscuits baked in one 9×13 dish.

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lbs (24 oz) chicken tenderloins, cooked and shredded
- 5 Pillsbury Grands biscuits, cut into 6 pieces each
- 1 can (10.5 oz) 98% fat free cream of chicken soup
- ½ cup Fairlife fat free milk
- ½ cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan (for inside)
- 4 tsp grated Parmesan (for topping)
- ½ cup reduced fat mozzarella
- 3 tsp Bella Sun Luci sun dried tomatoes (bagged)
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp Garlic Butter Seasoning (Target)
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼–½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Olive oil spray
- Fresh basil (optional)

## Steps
1. Cook and shred the chicken. Use the Instant Pot with broth and your favorite seasonings (poultry setting for 17 minutes), then lightly shred.
2. Preheat the oven to 375°F and spray a 9×13 baking dish.
3. Par-bake the biscuits. Cut each biscuit into 6 pieces, spread throughout the dish, and bake 10–12 minutes, until partially baked but not fully golden.
4. Make the sauce. Mix the cream of chicken soup, milk, Greek yogurt, ¼ cup Parmesan, minced garlic, and seasonings until creamy. Fold in the chicken and sun dried tomatoes.
5. Assemble. Spoon the chicken mixture around and over the partially baked biscuits.
6. Add the cheese. Top with 4 tsp Parmesan and ½ cup mozzarella.
7. Bake uncovered 20–25 minutes, until hot and bubbly, the cheese is starting to brown, and the biscuits are cooked through.
8. Rest 5–10 minutes. Top with fresh basil if you like.

## Notes
Nutrition per serving, as listed by the recipe author: 361 calories, 38 g protein, 30 g carbs, 10 g fat.
