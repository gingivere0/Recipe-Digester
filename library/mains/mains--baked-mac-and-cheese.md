# Mac and Cheese
Baked three-cheese mac and cheese, 25-30 minutes in the oven.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb corkscrew pasta
- 1 lb mozzarella (unshredded)
- 1 lb Colby Jack (unshredded)
- 8 oz sharp cheddar (unshredded)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp flour
- 12 oz evaporated milk
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard (ground mustard substitute)
- Chives (optional)

## Steps
1. Boil 1 lb pasta.
2. Shred 1 lb mozzarella, 1 lb Colby Jack, and 8 oz sharp cheddar. Toss it in a bowl. Set half aside.
3. Mix 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1 tsp garlic powder, and 1 tsp smoked paprika in a bowl.
4. Melt 3 tbsp butter in a pan on medium heat. Once melted, add half of the seasoning mix and stir. Mix in 3 tbsp flour.
5. Once combined, add 12 oz evaporated milk. Start whisking immediately so the flour doesn't clump.
6. Once thickened, add 2 cups heavy cream and mix. Then add the rest of the seasoning and stir.
7. Stir in 1 tbsp Dijon mustard.
8. Once it thickens a little, slowly add in half the cheese a handful at a time. Let the cheese melt before adding more.
9. Once half the cheese has been added, add in the pasta and stir until pasta is covered with the cheese sauce.
10. Layer the mac and cheese in a pan. Small layer of pasta on the bottom, then a generous layer of half of the remaining cheese, then layer remaining pasta, then top it with the rest of the cheese.
11. Put it in the oven at 350°F (175°C) for 25-30 minutes.
12. Optionally, broil for 2-3 minutes until top is golden brown and slightly crispy.
13. Optionally, add chives on top once it comes out of the oven.

## Notes
Use shredded cheese; the source lists it as "unshredded" but the steps say to shred it. The oven temperature is given as 350 in the source, with no unit.
