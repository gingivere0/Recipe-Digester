## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 cup butter, softened
- ¾ cup sugar
- 2 eggs

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt together.
3. Beat the butter and sugar until fluffy, then beat in the eggs.
4. Stir in the dry ingredients until just combined.
5. Bake spoonfuls for 9 to 11 minutes, until golden at the edges.

## Notes
Cookies keep for a week in an airtight tin.
